# Lanternway Brand Guide

## Name & tagline
- **Lanternway** — brand, app name, domain (lanternway.co / lanternway.ai).
- Store title: **Lanternway: Autism & ADHD Log**
- Taglines: *"Your child's whole journey, in one place."* · *"Track therapies, meds & IEPs — from preschool to adulthood."*

## Typography
Rounded, friendly sans-serif: **Nunito** (UI); wordmark uses a similar rounded teal sans.

## Logo
- Lantern with glowing amber heart, deep-teal outline, coral glow dashes.
- App icon: lantern on cream rounded square.
- Don't recolor, stretch, or add effects.

## Lumi (mascot)
- Small friendly flame who lives in the Lanternway lantern. Curled flame tip, little arms, dot eyes, small smile, coral blush, teal outline.
- **Do not restyle Lumi's shape.**
- Full body for illustrations; head-only icon for small sizes (notifications, loading, favicon).
- Always presented as "Lumi from Lanternway" — never a standalone product name.
- Moods: happy, cheering, sleepy, thinking, proud, comforting. Never sad, disappointed, or scolding.

## Voice
Warm, calm, plain English. Supportive, never judgmental. Celebrate wins; hold hard days gently. No medical advice. No puzzle pieces.
